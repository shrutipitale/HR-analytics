# 📊 Atlas Labs HR Analytics Dashboard

An interactive **HR Analytics Dashboard** built to analyze employee demographics, performance, and attrition trends.  
This dashboard provides actionable insights to help HR teams reduce attrition and improve workforce planning.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

The Atlas Labs Dashboard is divided into four main sections:

- **Overview**
- **Demographics**
- **Performance Tracker**
- **Attrition Analysis**

It enables HR teams to monitor employee metrics, identify risk factors, and make data-driven decisions.

## 📈 Key Business Insights

- Overtime significantly increases attrition rate.
- Employees with frequent travel show higher attrition.
- Early tenure employees (0–2 years) have higher churn.
- Sales & HR roles show higher attrition compared to Technology.
- Satisfaction levels directly correlate with retention.
